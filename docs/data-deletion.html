Data Deletion Instructions

News Scout, operated by The EWU Express (Eagle Production) · Last updated: 26 November 2026

News Scout does not store personal data of Facebook or Instagram users. It reads only public posts and aggregate engagement counts, and keeps a short-term record of reported stories (keywords and timestamps) for at most 48 hours.

If you manage a Page or account and want News Scout to stop accessing it

  1. Message The EWU Express on Facebook with the subject "News Scout data deletion" and the name of your Page or account.
  2. We will remove it from our monitoring list and delete any related cached records within 7 days, and confirm by reply.

If you authorised the News Scout app

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  2. Find News Scout and click Remove.
  3. This immediately revokes all access. Any tokens we held stop working.
