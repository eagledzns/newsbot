Privacy Policy

News Scout, operated by The EWU Express (Eagle Production) · Last updated: 26 November 2026

What News Scout is

News Scout is an internal newsroom tool operated by The EWU Express (Eagle Production). It monitors publicly available news and social media content to identify which stories are attracting the most public attention, so our editors can decide what to cover. It posts its findings only to our own private Telegram channel.

Information we access

  • Public news content: headlines, links and publication times from news websites and RSS feeds.
  • Public trend data: Google Trends, Google News, Wikipedia page-view statistics and public YouTube video statistics.
  • Facebook and Instagram (via Meta's official Graph API): public posts and aggregate engagement counts (reactions, comments count, shares, likes) from Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts, including Pages and accounts that we manage ourselves.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of individual Facebook or Instagram users.
  • We do not read private messages, comment text authors, friend lists or any non-public content.
  • We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or sale to third parties.
  • News Scout does not offer a login to the public and has no end users other than our editorial team.

How we use the information

Engagement counts are used only to calculate a "virality score" for news stories. Alerts containing a headline, a link to the original public post or article, and the aggregate engagement numbers are sent to our internal Telegram channel.

Storage and retention

News Scout keeps only a short-term memory of which stories it has already reported (story keywords and timestamps) for up to 48 hours to avoid duplicates. Cached article titles are deleted after 3 days. No Facebook or Instagram user data is stored.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Alerts are visible only to members of our internal Telegram channel.

Security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never published. The tool uses read-only permissions and cannot post, comment or message on any platform.
